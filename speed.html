📚 About Speed Conversion

Speed tells us how quickly an object moves from one place to another. Different countries and industries use different speed units.

🚗 Common Speed Units

  • Kilometers per hour (km/h): Commonly used for vehicle speed in many countries.
  • Miles per hour (mph): Commonly used in the United States and United Kingdom.
  • Meters per second (m/s): Commonly used in science and physics.
  • Knots: Commonly used in aviation and marine navigation.
  • Feet per second (ft/s): Used in some engineering and scientific applications.

🔄 Common Conversions

  • 1 km/h = 0.621371 mph
  • 1 mph = 1.609344 km/h
  • 1 m/s = 3.6 km/h
  • 1 knot = 1.852 km/h
  • 1 ft/s = 1.09728 km/h

📝 How to Use

  1. Enter a speed value.
  2. Select the unit you are converting from.
  3. Select the unit you want to convert to.
  4. Tap Convert.

💡 Example

If you convert 100 km/h to mph, the result is approximately 62.1371 mph.
