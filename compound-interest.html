📚 What is Compound Interest?

Compound interest is interest calculated on the original principal and also on the interest accumulated from previous periods.

📐 Formula

A = P × (1 + r/n)^(nt)

Where:
P = Principal amount
r = Annual interest rate
n = Number of compounding periods per year
t = Time in years
A = Final amount

💡 Example

If you invest ₹10,000 at 10% annual interest for 2 years, compounded annually, the calculator will calculate the final amount and compound interest.
