What is EMI?

EMI stands for Equated Monthly Installment. It is the fixed amount paid every month toward a loan.

The EMI depends on the loan amount, interest rate and loan tenure.

This calculator provides an estimate. Actual loan payments may vary depending on the lender and loan terms.
