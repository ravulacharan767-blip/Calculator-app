What is EMI?

EMI stands for Equated Monthly Instalment. It is the fixed amount you generally pay every month toward a loan. The EMI includes both the principal amount and the interest charged by the lender.


How is EMI calculated?

The calculator uses the loan amount, annual interest rate and loan tenure to calculate the monthly payment. The total interest is then calculated from the total payments over the loan period.


Understanding your result

Monthly EMI is the estimated amount payable each month. Total Interest is the estimated interest paid over the full loan period. Total Payment is the principal plus the total interest.
