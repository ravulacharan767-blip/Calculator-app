Estimate an adult reference weight range based on height.

Estimated Adult Reference Range

This range is calculated using adult BMI reference values and your height. It is not a personal ideal-weight target.

⚠️ IMPORTANT FOR CHILDREN & TEENAGERS This calculator does not calculate a target or reference weight for people under 18. Children and teenagers are still growing, and weight assessment uses age- and sex-specific growth information. If you have concerns about growth or weight, speak with a parent, doctor, or qualified healthcare professional.

❓ What is Calculate?

For adults, this calculator estimates a reference weight range from height using BMI values of 18.5 and 24.9.

BMI is calculated from height and weight. The resulting range should be treated as a general reference rather than a definition of a person's healthy or ideal body.

For people under 18, adult BMI reference ranges are not used because growth and development need to be considered.
