What is a Loan Calculator?

A loan calculator estimates your monthly payment based on the loan amount, interest rate and repayment period.

It also shows the estimated total interest and the total amount you would repay over the loan period.

The figures are estimates. Actual loan terms may vary depending on the lender and loan agreement.
