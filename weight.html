⚖️ About Weight Converter

A Weight Converter helps you convert a measurement from one weight unit to another quickly and accurately. It can be useful for school, cooking, shopping, science, engineering, and everyday measurements.

What is Weight?

Weight and mass are often used interchangeably in everyday conversation. Common measurement units include kilograms, grams, pounds, and ounces.

Common Weight Units

  • Kilogram (kg) – Commonly used for measuring body weight and larger objects.
  • Gram (g) – Used for smaller quantities.
  • Milligram (mg) – Used for very small quantities.
  • Tonne (t) – Used for very large masses.
  • Pound (lb) – Commonly used in the imperial system.
  • Ounce (oz) – Used for smaller measurements in the imperial system.
  • Stone (st) – A unit equal to 14 pounds.

Common Conversions

1 kilogram = 1,000 grams
1 gram = 1,000 milligrams
1 tonne = 1,000 kilograms
1 kilogram = 2.20462 pounds
1 pound = 16 ounces
1 stone = 14 pounds

How to Use the Weight Converter

  1. Enter the value you want to convert.
  2. Select the unit you are converting from.
  3. Select the unit you want to convert to.
  4. Click the Convert button.
  5. Your converted value will appear instantly.

Example

To convert 10 kilograms into pounds, enter 10, select Kilogram as From, select Pound as To, and click Convert.
