📚 What is an Age Calculator?

An age calculator determines how much time has passed between a person's date of birth and today's date.

It can display your age in years, months and days and can also calculate the approximate total number of days, weeks and months.

📅 How Does Age Calculation Work?

The calculator compares your date of birth with the current date.

Current Date − Date of Birth = Age

The calculator accounts for different month lengths when calculating the years, months and days.

✨ What Can You Calculate?

  • Exact age in years
  • Remaining months and days
  • Total months
  • Total weeks
  • Total days
  • Days remaining until your next birthday

ℹ️ Important Information

The calculator uses your device's current date when determining your age and next birthday.

Total weeks are calculated from the total number of elapsed days and may include a partial week.
