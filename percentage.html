What is a Percentage?

A percentage is a way of expressing a number as a fraction of 100. The symbol used for percentage is %.

Percentage = (Part ÷ Whole) × 100

Percentages are commonly used for discounts, marks, interest rates, statistics, increases and decreases.

Percentage Examples

20% of 200 = 40

25 is what percentage of 100? = 25%

Increase from 100 to 120 = 20%

Where Are Percentages Used?

Percentages are used in many everyday situations, including:

Discounts
Exam Marks
Interest Rates
Business Statistics
Population Statistics
Price Changes
Tip: Always check whether you are calculating a percentage of a number, finding what percentage one number is of another, or calculating a percentage change.
