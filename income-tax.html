📚 About this Calculator

This calculator provides an estimate using the new tax regime for AY 2026–27.

🧮 New Tax Regime Slabs

  • Up to ₹4,00,000 — Nil
  • ₹4,00,001 to ₹8,00,000 — 5%
  • ₹8,00,001 to ₹12,00,000 — 10%
  • ₹12,00,001 to ₹16,00,000 — 15%
  • ₹16,00,001 to ₹20,00,000 — 20%
  • ₹20,00,001 to ₹24,00,000 — 25%
  • Above ₹24,00,000 — 30%

💡 Standard Deduction

Salaried individuals and pensioners can have a ₹75,000 standard deduction under the new regime.

This is an estimated calculator and does not cover every deduction, exemption, surcharge, special-rate income, or individual tax situation.
