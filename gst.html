📚 What is GST?

GST stands for Goods and Services Tax. It is a tax applied to many goods and services.

🧮 How is GST calculated?

When adding GST:

GST Amount = Amount × GST Rate ÷ 100

Total Amount = Amount + GST Amount

When removing GST from a GST-inclusive price:

Original Amount = Inclusive Amount ÷ (1 + GST Rate ÷ 100)

GST Amount = Inclusive Amount − Original Amount

💡 Example

If the amount is ₹1,000 and GST is 18%:

  • GST = ₹180
  • Total = ₹1,180
