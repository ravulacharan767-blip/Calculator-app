What is SIP?

SIP stands for Systematic Investment Plan. It allows investors to invest a fixed amount regularly, usually every month, into a mutual fund or investment scheme.

How does this calculator work?

This calculator estimates the future value of your monthly SIP investment using the monthly investment, expected annual return and investment period.

Important

The result is an estimate based on the return rate you enter. Actual investment returns can be different.
