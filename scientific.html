❓ What is a Scientific Calculator?

A scientific calculator performs basic arithmetic as well as advanced mathematical operations.

This calculator supports trigonometric functions, logarithms, powers, square roots, constants such as π and e, parentheses, and standard arithmetic.

Trigonometric functions use degrees.
