📚 What is a Mortgage?

A mortgage is a loan used to purchase a home or other property. The borrower generally repays the loan through regular payments over a specified period.

🧮 How is the payment calculated?

The monthly payment depends on the loan amount, interest rate, and loan term.

This calculator uses the standard fixed-payment loan formula.

💡 Example

Try entering:

  • Loan Amount: ₹10,00,000
  • Interest Rate: 8%
  • Loan Term: 10 years

The calculator will show the estimated monthly payment, total payment, and total interest.

⚠️ Important

This is an estimate. Actual mortgage payments can also include taxes, insurance, fees, and other charges depending on the loan.
