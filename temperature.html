🌡️ Temperature Converter

🌡️ About Temperature Converter

A Temperature Converter helps you convert temperatures between Celsius, Fahrenheit, and Kelvin. It is useful for school, science, weather, cooking, engineering, and everyday measurements.

What is Temperature?

Temperature describes how hot or cold something is. Different temperature scales are used around the world and in different scientific applications.

Common Temperature Units

  • Celsius (°C) – Commonly used in most countries for everyday temperature measurements.
  • Fahrenheit (°F) – Commonly used for everyday temperatures in the United States.
  • Kelvin (K) – An SI temperature scale commonly used in science.

Common Conversions

0°C = 32°F
100°C = 212°F
0°C = 273.15 K
100°C = 373.15 K
32°F = 0°C
212°F = 100°C

How to Use the Temperature Converter

  1. Enter the temperature value.
  2. Select the temperature unit you are converting from.
  3. Select the temperature unit you want to convert to.
  4. Click the Convert button.
  5. Your converted temperature will appear instantly.

Example

To convert 100°C into Fahrenheit, enter 100, select Celsius as From, select Fahrenheit as To, and click Convert.
