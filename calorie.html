❓ What is Calculate?

This calculator estimates the amount of energy your body may need each day based on age, gender, height, weight and activity level.

For adults, the calculator uses an established resting-energy equation together with an activity factor.

For people under 18, calorie requirements are estimated using age-specific equations because children and teenagers are still growing.

⚠️ This is an estimate for informational purposes, not a medical or dietary prescription. Children and teenagers have individual growth and nutrition needs, so calorie targets should not be used to restrict food without guidance from a parent, doctor or qualified dietitian.
