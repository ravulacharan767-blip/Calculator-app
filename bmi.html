What is BMI?

BMI stands for Body Mass Index. It is a numerical value calculated using a person's weight and height.

BMI is commonly used as a screening measurement to help assess weight in relation to height.

BMI = Weight (kg) ÷ Height² (m²)

How is BMI Calculated?

To calculate BMI, height is first converted from centimeters to meters.

The weight in kilograms is then divided by the height in meters squared.

Example: 70 kg ÷ (1.75 × 1.75) = 22.9

Adult BMI Categories

For adults, BMI is commonly grouped into the following categories:

BMI Category
Below 18.5 Underweight
18.5 – 24.9 Healthy weight
25.0 – 29.9 Overweight
30.0 or higher Obesity

BMI for Children & Teenagers

BMI is interpreted differently for children and teenagers.

For ages 2–19, BMI is compared with growth charts using age and sex. Adult BMI categories should not be used for children and teenagers.

Important: This calculator provides the BMI number, but it does not calculate a child or teenager's BMI-for-age percentile.

Why is BMI Used?

  • It is quick and simple to calculate.
  • It uses only height and weight.
  • It can be used as a general screening measurement.
  • It can help identify people who may need further assessment.

Limitations of BMI

BMI does not directly measure body fat.

  • It does not measure body-fat percentage.
  • It does not show where body fat is stored.
  • It does not account for muscle mass.
  • Age and sex affect how BMI should be interpreted.
Remember: BMI is a screening tool and is not a diagnosis of an individual's health.
