What is BMI?

BMI stands for Body Mass Index. It is a number calculated from a person's height and weight.

BMI can be used as a screening measurement, but it does not directly measure body fat or provide a complete picture of someone's health.

BMI = Weight (kg) ÷ Height² (m²)

How is BMI calculated?

First, height in centimeters is converted into meters. Then the weight in kilograms is divided by height in meters squared.

Adult BMI Categories

  • Below 18.5 — Underweight
  • 18.5 to 24.9 — Healthy weight
  • 25.0 to 29.9 — Overweight
  • 30.0 or higher — Obesity
Important: These categories are for adults. For children and teenagers, BMI is interpreted using age- and sex-specific growth charts rather than adult BMI categories.

Things to Know About BMI

  • BMI is a screening measurement, not a diagnosis.
  • BMI does not directly measure body fat.
  • Age and sex can affect how BMI should be interpreted.
  • For children and teenagers, growth charts are used.
  • Other health and lifestyle factors are also important.
