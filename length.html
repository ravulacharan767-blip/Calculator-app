📏 About Length Converter

A Length Converter helps you convert measurements from one length unit to another quickly and accurately. It can be useful for school, engineering, construction, travel, shopping, and everyday measurements.

What is Length?

Length is a measurement of the distance between two points. Different measurement systems use different units to measure length.

Common Length Units

  • Meter (m) – Standard SI unit of length.
  • Kilometer (km) – Used for long distances.
  • Centimeter (cm) – Used for smaller measurements.
  • Millimeter (mm) – Used for very small measurements.
  • Mile (mi) – Used for longer distances.
  • Yard (yd) – Used in some sports and measurements.
  • Foot (ft) – Commonly used for height.
  • Inch (in) – Used for small measurements.

Common Conversions

1 kilometer = 1,000 meters
1 meter = 100 centimeters
1 meter = 1,000 millimeters
1 foot = 12 inches
1 yard = 3 feet
1 mile = 1,609.344 meters

How to Use the Length Converter

  1. Enter the value you want to convert.
  2. Select the unit you are converting from.
  3. Select the unit you want to convert to.
  4. Click the Convert button.
  5. Your converted value will appear instantly.

Example

To convert 5 kilometers into meters, enter 5, select Kilometer as From, select Meter as To, and click Convert.
